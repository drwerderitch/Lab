How the Throw Works
After you aim (click your player, or use the Throw button), hold LEFT SHIFT to charge power and release it to start typing. The longer you hold, the farther your throw can go — but releasing a bigger charge gives you less time to type, so a huge throw is riskier to land cleanly.
Always start with A
Each key after that bends the flight. Top row = curves one way. Home row = straight. Bottom row = curves the other way. Keys must move rightward across the keyboard, row to row or within a row. The number of keys you land also sets your distance — more keys, farther throw.
